# DEX

**Turn any image, PDF or sound into what you need.** A small, native Mac app that keeps quality wherever it can and tells you plainly when it can't.

<p align="center"><img src="design/screenshot.jpg" width="800" alt="DEX with a photo open"></p>

## What it does
- **Convert** images between JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, GIF and PDF.
- **Crop**, rotate and **Fit** to any shape.
- **Enhance** with AI: 2× or 4× sharper, for photos, anime and faces.
- **Compose** images together, or design on a page (A4, Story, any size).
- **Animations:** trim GIFs and save single frames.
- **PDF:** make, split, merge and edit PDFs.
- **Audio:** trim and convert, or download from a YouTube link.

## Install
1. Download **DEX.zip** from [Releases](https://github.com/vxkram/DEX/releases/latest) and move **DEX.app** to Applications.
2. The first time, allow it in **System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway** (it isn't notarised yet).

Needs an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later. To build it yourself, see [docs/DEVELOPMENT.md](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md).

## Privacy
Everything runs on your Mac and nothing is uploaded. DEX only goes online when you ask it to: a YouTube download, or the one-time download of an Enhance model.
